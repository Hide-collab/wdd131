01 Site Name

Zimbabwe Travel & Culture Guide

This name clearly communicates the site's dual focus: travel (destinations, tips, and attractions) and culture (traditions, food, history, and events). It is straightforward, inclusive, and immediately tells visitors what they will find on the site without being limiting.

The word Zimbabwe anchors the brand geographically, while Guide positions the site as a helpful, informative resource rather than just a tourism advertisement.

02 Site Purpose

The Zimbabwe Travel & Culture Guide is a multi-page informational website designed to introduce Zimbabwe's rich heritage, breathtaking landscapes, and vibrant culture to both local and international audiences.

The site will feature dedicated pages for major tourist attractions such as Victoria Falls, the Great Zimbabwe monument, Hwange National Park, and the Eastern Highlands. Additional pages will cover traditional cuisine, cultural events, music, and festivals. Practical travel tips — such as visa information, currency, and transportation — will also be included.

Interactive JavaScript features will include an image gallery, a filterable list of attractions, and call-to-action buttons that guide visitors to explore deeper content. The goal is to give anyone planning or simply curious about a trip to Zimbabwe a comprehensive, visually engaging starting point.

03 Scenarios

The following questions represent common inquiries from the site's target audience — travellers, students, and people curious about Zimbabwean culture:

  • What are the top tourist attractions I should visit in Zimbabwe, and how do I get there?
  • What traditional Zimbabwean foods should I try, and where can I find them?
  • What cultural events or festivals take place in Zimbabwe throughout the year?
  • What practical travel information do I need before visiting Zimbabwe — such as visa requirements, safety tips, and currency?

04 Color Schema

The palette draws inspiration from Zimbabwe's natural landscape — the rich red soils of Mashonaland, the golden savannas at dusk, the deep greens of Nyanga's forests, and the warm cream of dried grass. Every colour has a clear role:

Earth Brown #8B4513 Used for: headings, logo, primary links, section borders
Savanna Gold #C9922B Used for: accent highlights, call-to-action buttons, badges, dividers
Baobab Green #4A6741 Used for: navigation bar background, category tags, footer accents
Paper Cream #FDF6EC Used for: main page background, card backgrounds, form fields
Deep Wood #1C1209 Used for: body text, header/footer background, primary dark contrast
Dusty Path #7A6650 Used for: captions, metadata labels, secondary descriptive text

05 Typography

Two complementary Google Fonts are selected to balance elegance with readability:

Font 1 — Cormorant Garamond (Display / Serif)

Victoria Falls — Wonder of the World

Used for: page titles (h1), section headings (h2, h3), pull quotes, hero text, and the site logo wordmark.
Weights used: 400 (regular), 600 (semi-bold), 700 (bold), 400 italic.

Font 2 — DM Sans (Body / Sans-serif)

Zimbabwe is home to some of Africa's most spectacular landscapes, from the thundering waters of Victoria Falls to the ancient stone walls of Great Zimbabwe. Whether you are planning a safari in Hwange or exploring the vibrant markets of Harare, this guide has everything you need.

Used for: all body paragraphs, navigation links, button labels, captions, form inputs, and general UI text.
Weights used: 300 (light), 400 (regular), 500 (medium).
