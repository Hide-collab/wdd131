About Zimbabwe

Zimbabwe is a landlocked nation in southern Africa, bordered by Zambia, Mozambique, South Africa, and Botswana. It is celebrated for Victoria Falls — one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World — as well as the ancient stone city of Great Zimbabwe, vast wildlife reserves, and a rich cultural heritage spanning thousands of years.

The country covers approximately 390,757 km² and is home to around 15 million people who speak over 16 languages, with Shona and Ndebele being the most widely spoken. Harare is the vibrant capital city, while Bulawayo serves as the cultural heart of the Ndebele people.

Highlights

  • Victoria Falls

    The world's largest waterfall by combined width and height — 1,708 m wide and 108 m tall.

  • Great Zimbabwe Ruins

    A UNESCO World Heritage Site and the largest stone structure in sub-Saharan Africa, dating to the 11th century.

  • Hwange National Park

    Zimbabwe's largest game reserve, protecting over 100 mammal species including Africa's largest elephant population.

  • 🎨
    Shona Stone Sculpture

    World-renowned for its Shona sculptors whose work is displayed in galleries across the globe.
